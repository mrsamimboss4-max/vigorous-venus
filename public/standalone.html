Help & Support Center

Frequently asked questions and guides for Windows PC and Android devices.

WINDOWS PC

Where does Windows save screenshots by default?

Windows typically saves captures made via Win + PrtScn or Snipping Tool inside C:\Users\<Username>\Pictures\Screenshots. Sweepgrid auto-detects this folder on startup, or lets you choose custom directories.

WINDOWS PC

Are deleted screenshots recoverable on Windows?

Yes. By default, Sweepgrid sends deleted files to the native Windows Recycle Bin. You can easily open your Recycle Bin to restore any image accidentally deleted.

ANDROID MOBILE

How do I install the Android APK?

Download the APK directly via our download button. When prompted, enable 'Allow from this source' in your device settings to complete installation. Sweepgrid requires no root access.

ANDROID MOBILE

How does the Android 30-Day Trash safeguard work?

On Android 10 and above, Sweepgrid moves deleted screenshots to the official Android MediaStore Trash. Items are retained for 30 days and can be restored at any time from Google Photos or your device Gallery.

PRIVACY & OFFLINE

Is any personal data uploaded to cloud servers?

No. Sweepgrid runs 100% locally on your hardware. There is zero telemetry, zero user tracking, and no external data sync.
