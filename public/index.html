Help & Support

Quick answers and troubleshooting guides for Sweepgrid.

How do I select custom screenshot directories?

Upon launching Sweepgrid, click the folder picker icon in the upper navigation bar to target any directory on your local drives containing your image captures. Sweepgrid remembers your favorite paths between sessions.

Is my data uploaded to the cloud?

No. Sweepgrid is an entirely local desktop utility. All scanning, sorting, and deletions happen directly on your local storage.

Where does Windows save screenshots by default?

Windows typically saves captures made via Win + PrtScn or Snipping Tool inside C:\Users\<Username>\Pictures\Screenshots. Sweepgrid auto-detects this folder on startup.

Are deleted screenshots recoverable?

Yes. Sweepgrid sends deleted files to the native Windows Recycle Bin by default, allowing you to restore any accidentally selected capture.

You can reach out directly via the Contact page or submit issues through our official developer channels.
